Administrator

Built for the way teams actually work

Each module stays deliberately small and legible, so onboarding a new teammate takes minutes rather than a full afternoon of training.

Structured records

Every entry carries an identifier, owner, region and status, so filtering and sorting stay predictable no matter how large the portfolio grows.

Managed access

Invite teammates, assign one of three roles, and suspend accounts instantly. Every change is recorded against the member it affects.

Auditable activity

A rolling activity feed captures who approved, exported or flagged each record, giving reviewers a defensible history without extra tooling.

One-click export

Export the current filtered view to CSV for reporting, and share a consistent snapshot with people who sit outside the console.

Session protection

Signed sessions expire automatically, and protected pages redirect to sign in with a clear return path once the session ends.

One design system

Colors, spacing and typography come from shared tokens, keeping every screen consistent whether it is opened on a laptop or a phone.

Role capabilities Three tiers
Administrator Full access

Manages members and roles, exports data, and reviews every record in all regions.

Analyst Operational access

Works records across regions, approves changes, and exports reports for the team.

Viewer Read only

Reviews records and activity without the ability to approve, export, or edit.
